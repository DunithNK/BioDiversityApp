<h1>WildSense Gal Oya – Intelligent Wildlife Monitoring &amp; Conservation System</h1>

<p>
  WildSense Gal Oya is an AI-powered, mobile-first wildlife monitoring and conservation support system designed for
  <strong>Gal Oya National Park, Sri Lanka</strong>.
  The platform integrates four specialized intelligent components into a single ecosystem to support
  <strong>wildlife health assessment, species detection, population monitoring, and conservation decision-making</strong>,
  with a primary focus on <strong>Sri Lankan Leopards and Sloth Bears</strong>.
</p>

<p>
  The system addresses critical challenges such as limited visibility in dense forests, subjective wildlife health
  assessments, high-cost monitoring methods, and lack of structured long-term conservation data.
  WildSense Gal Oya enables <strong>rangers, wildlife officers, researchers, and eco-tourism stakeholders</strong>
  to make data-driven, objective decisions using AI and field-friendly technologies.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>Features</h2>

<h3>ThermalVital Monitor – AI-Based Thermal Stress Assessment</h3>
<p>
  An intelligent thermal analysis system that converts thermal images into a
  <strong>Thermal Stress Index (TSI)</strong> for injured leopards and sloth bears.
</p>
<ul>
  <li>Objective health indicator for treatment and release decisions</li>
  <li>AI-trained species-specific thermal models</li>
  <li>Enables thermal-based recovery monitoring over time</li>
  <li>Reduces reliance on subjective visual judgment</li>
</ul>

<h3>Eco-Acoustic Detection &amp; Species Classification</h3>
<p>
  A long-range wildlife detection system using <strong>parabolic microphones</strong> and AI-based sound analysis
  to identify leopard and sloth bear vocalizations.
</p>
<ul>
  <li>Detects animal presence in dense forest areas</li>
  <li>Displays activity hotspots on a GPS-based map</li>
  <li>Supports wildlife guides and eco-tourism operations</li>
  <li>Non-visual, low-disturbance monitoring approach</li>
</ul>

<h3>Collaborative Health &amp; Sighting Tracker</h3>
<p>
  A structured, mobile-based <strong>citizen-science and ranger collaboration platform</strong>
  for logging wildlife sightings with health and behavior indicators.
</p>
<ul>
  <li>Photo/video-based smart sighting logs</li>
  <li>Structured health and behavior inputs</li>
  <li>AI-assisted triage for urgency detection</li>
  <li>Offline-first support for remote park areas</li>
  <li>Map-based dashboard with color-coded alerts (Healthy / Concern / Critical)</li>
</ul>

<h3>AI Wildlife Sign &amp; Spoor Tracker</h3>
<p>
  A mobile AI system that identifies wildlife species using
  <strong>footprints and physical signs</strong>, enabling non-invasive population monitoring.
</p>
<ul>
  <li>Identifies species from footprints and signs</li>
  <li>Works fully offline on mobile devices</li>
  <li>Cost-effective alternative to camera traps</li>
  <li>Enables standardized identification by non-experts</li>
</ul>
